## Overview

GovConnect was our fourth-year thesis project — a content management system built for government websites. The goal was to give LGUs a practical way to manage public-facing content without needing a full custom rebuild every time.

## My Contributions

As **sole developer** and **team lead**, I handled architecture, implementation, and coordination with my group. I built the full PERN stack application and designed the system so other LGUs could adapt it for their own use.

## Key Features

- Editable content modules for government pages
- Adaptable configuration for different LGU needs
- Full-stack architecture with PostgreSQL, Express, React, and Node.js

## Takeaways

- Learned to design systems meant for reuse, not just a single deployment
- Strengthened leadership and delivery skills on a high-stakes capstone project
- Validated the work through a **College Best Thesis** award in Governance
